# Design

The interface follows the Coffee Delivery design in Figma. The light theme is the only theme: do not build or ship a dark theme. The visual direction is warm and friendly: a light neutral background, a purple and yellow brand, rounded cards with one distinctive asymmetric corner, round product photos, no shadows.

## Source of truth

- The Figma file "Coffee Delivery - Desafio React (Copy)" has four desktop screens, each 1440 px wide on a `#FAFAFA` background: Home (1440×2293), Checkout, Checkout Filled, and Success (1440×1013).
- `docs/design/figma-analysis.md` records every validated token, component, measure, and text. When the render and the Figma file's paint data disagree, the render wins, and the analysis lists the corrections.
- The design text is in Portuguese and its prices are in reais. The product uses the English text and the US dollar amounts defined in [BUSINESS.md](BUSINESS.md) and [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md).
- The design shows no hover, focus, disabled, error, or selected-filter states for most components. Specs define them from the tokens and record them in [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `/` | Navbar, hero (title, subtitle, four benefits, cup image), "Our coffees" with tag filters, grid of coffee cards |
| Checkout | `/checkout` | Left column "Complete your order" with the delivery address card and the payment card; right column "Selected coffees" with the order summary |
| Checkout Filled | `/checkout` | The same screen with the form filled and credit card selected |
| Success | `/orders/:orderId` | Confirmation title, order card (address, delivery estimate, payment), delivery illustration |

## Layout rules (desktop, 1440 px)

- Page margin 160 px, so the content is 1120 px wide. Navbar 104 px high on white (spec 016): the logo starts at x=160 (85×40), the location pill is 38 px high and the cart link 38×38 ends at x=1280, 12 px apart; the badge is a 20×20 circle on the top right corner of the cart link. Every page is wrapped by `AppLayout` (skip link, navbar, `main#main-content`).
- Hero (spec 017, `features/home/hero`): 544 px high under the navbar (spec 029 measured the frame), 92 px of padding above and below the content. The intro column is 544 px wide at x=160: the h1 (Title XL, `title`), 16 px below it the subtitle (Text Regular L, `subtitle`), 66 px below that the benefits in a 2×2 grid (40 px between columns, 20 between rows, each text on one line, so the grid is 570 px wide). Each benefit is a 32 px circle with a white 16 px icon, 12 px from its text (Text Regular M, `text`): purchase `yellow-dark`, packaging `text`, delivery `yellow`, freshness `purple`. The cup image is 476×360, ends at x=1280, and is vertically centered; the intro sits 8 px above the center (measured against the frame, spec 029). Behind them, three soft shapes (`hero-blob` at 30% and 25%, `purple-light`) blurred by 100 px; they are styles, not images, and bleed below the section.
- Home list (spec 018, `features/catalog`): under the hero, a `section` with 32 px above the heading "Our coffees" (Title L, `subtitle`), 54 px between the heading and the first row, and 154 px below the last row (the full home page is 2294 px high at 1440 px; the Figma frame is 2293). The grid is four columns of 256 px cards, 32 px between columns, 40 px between rows, 1120 px in all. The last row has two cards, aligned to the left.
- Filter chips (spec 019; the design has a hidden frame here, so the look is proposed, not measured): five chips on the title row, at its right, ending at x=1280, 8 px apart, centered on the line of the title. A chip is the tag shape (radius 100, 12 px Roboto Bold, uppercase, 24 px high, 12 px of side padding): idle has a 1 px `yellow` border and `yellow-dark` text; selected has a `yellow` fill and white text. While at least one chip is selected, a secondary "Clear filters" button shows 8 px before the first chip, so the chips do not move when it appears. In the filtered empty state the message "No coffees match the selected filters." and a "Clear filters" button replace the grid.
- Coffee card (spec 018): 256×310, `card` fill, corners 6/36/6/36, 24 px side padding and 20 px bottom padding (measured against the frame, spec 029: the price starts and the cart button ends 24 px from the card edge). From the top: the round 120 px photo, lifted 20 px over the top edge and centered; 12 px; the tags, centered, 4 px apart; 16 px; the name (Title S, `subtitle`); 8 px; the description (Text Regular S, `label`, centered, up to three lines); the purchase row at the bottom, 38 px high, with the price at the left (a small "$" in Text Regular S and the amount in Subtitle XL, both `text`). At the right of the row (spec 021): the counter, 72×38 with purple plus and minus icons, then the cart button, 38×38, `purple-dark` fill, white cart icon, radius 6, 8 px apart; the button ends on the 24 px padding of the card (measured at 1440 px: the counter and the button are on one line, 8 px apart, 38 px high). The counter starts at 1 and keeps its value after an addition, so the visitor sees what was just added; a badge (the `Badge` component, `aria-hidden`) on the top right corner of the cart button, out of the flow so the card keeps its size, says how many of that coffee are in the cart, and is absent while there are none (the design does not show either; this was asked after the spec 021 choice of resetting to 1). Adding does not open the cart, a toast, or a drawer: the badge on the navbar grows (the sum of the quantities) and a hidden polite message says, for example, "Added 2 × Latte to cart".
- Checkout (spec 022, `pages/CheckoutPage.tsx`): a grid of two fixed columns, 640 px at x=160 ("Complete your order") and 448 px at x=832 ("Selected coffees"), 32 px apart, 39 px under the navbar; both headings are Title XS in `subtitle`. A visually hidden level 1 "Checkout" comes first. The left column has its heading and, under it 16 px, the address card (spec 023) and, 12 px below it, the payment card (spec 024).
- Address card (spec 023, `features/checkout/address-card`): `card` fill, radius 6, 40 px padding, 640 px wide, so the fields are 560 px wide. The header is the pin icon (`map-pin-line`, 22 px, `yellow-dark`), 8 px, the title "Delivery address" (Roboto 16, `subtitle`) and under it, 4.8 px lower than the line height of the style gives (the design's line height is 1.6), "Enter the address where you want to receive your order" (Text Regular S, `text`); the fields start 32 px below that. Seven inputs, 42 px high, 16 px between rows and 12 px between columns: ZIP code 200; Street 560; Number 200 with Complement 348 (the hint "Optional" in 12 px italic); Neighborhood 200 with City 276 and State 60. The placeholders are the names ("ZIP code", "Street", "Number", "Complement", "Neighborhood", "City", "State"); each field also has a visually hidden label of the same text. The border is `#E6E5E5` idle and `#C47F17` when focused or filled; an invalid field has a `#CC2937` border and its message below it in `error` (Text Regular S). A message is shown when a field loses focus, then it follows the value; a submit shows all of them. The State message ("Enter the 2-letter state, like RS") wraps on four lines in its 60 px column: the design fixes the width and the spec fixes the text.
- Payment card (spec 024, `features/checkout/payment-card`): the same card as the address card (`card` fill, radius 6, 40 px padding, 640 px wide), with a purple `currency-dollar` icon, the title "Payment" and the line "Payment is made on delivery. Choose the way you want to pay"; 32 px below, a row of options, 12 px apart, each about 179×51 (the 560 px inner width split in three): an icon in `purple`, 16 px of padding, 12 px between icon and label, the label in Button S (Roboto 12, uppercase). The options are "Credit card" (`credit-card`), "Debit card" (`bank`), and "Cash" (`money`), one for each method the checkout config returns, in its order. Idle: `button` fill and border; selected: `purple-light` fill and a 1 px `purple` border; nothing is selected at first. A submit with no choice shows "Choose a payment method" under the row, in `error`. While the config loads the card shows "Loading payment methods…", and when it fails "We couldn't load the payment methods." with "Try again" (the design has no frame for these).
- Order confirmation (spec 026, `pages/OrderPage.tsx`, `features/orders/order-confirmation`): content on the 160 px margin, 80 px under the navbar. The h1 "Uhu! Order confirmed" (Title L, `yellow-dark`, 1 px lower than the style gives), the line "Now just wait and your coffee will soon be with you" (Text Regular L, `subtitle`, 2 px under the title), and 40 px below, a grid of the order card (526 px) and the illustration (492×293, empty `alt`) with the illustration ending on x=1280; the drawing starts at the top edge of the card, and the picture has 12 px of empty space above the drawing, so the picture starts 12 px above the card (measured against the frame, spec 029). The card is about 526×270: a 1 px gradient border from `yellow` to `purple` (a wrapper with the gradient and 1 px of padding around an inner card whose corners are 1 px smaller, so the border keeps the 6/36/6/36 shape), a `background` inner fill, 40 px padding, and three rows 32 px apart, each a 32 px round icon (white, 16 px) and two lines of Text Regular M in `text`, 12 px from the icon: a `purple` pin with "Delivery to **<street>, <number>**" and "<neighborhood> - <city>, <state>"; a `yellow` timer with "Estimated delivery" and **"<min> min - <max> min"** (from `estimatedDeliveryMinutes`); a `yellow-dark` dollar with "Payment on delivery" and **the label of the method** ("Credit card", "Debit card", or "Cash", from the app's map). The complement, the items, and the prices are not shown. Loading shows "Loading your order…"; an id the API does not know (a `404`, also for a malformed id) shows the h1 "Order not found" (Title L, `subtitle`) and the link "Back to coffees" to `/` (Button G style in `purple-dark`, underlined); another failure shows "We couldn't load your order." with "Try again". The design has no frame for these three states. When the order's address was cleared after the retention period (spec 038), the first row of the card reads "The address was removed after 90 days." in place of the delivery lines; the design has no frame for it either.
- Summary card (spec 022, `features/checkout/order-summary`): `card` fill, corners 6/44/6/44, 40 px padding, so the content is 368 px wide. Each row is 80 px high (a 64 px round photo, 20 px, then the name in Text Regular M `subtitle` with the compact counter 72×32 and the secondary "Remove" button, 32 px high with the trash icon, 8 px apart, and the line price at the right in Text Bold M `text`; the text and the controls start 2 px below the top of the photo), then 24 px, a 1 px `button` divider, and 23 px before the next row, so the rows are 128 px apart as in the frame. Then 24 px, "Items total" and "Delivery" (label Text Regular S, value Text Regular M, both `text`), 12 px apart, and "Total" in Text Bold L `subtitle` (20 px, label and value), then 24 px and the full-width "Confirm order" button, 368×46, `yellow` fill. The Figma label is "REMOVER" (91 px wide); the English "Remove" is about 83 px wide with the same padding, icon, and gap, so the width is not forced. The Figma mock shows Latte with a counter of 1 and a price of 19.80; the line prices and the totals are the ones of the quote of the server (`POST /api/v1/orders/quote`, spec 039), which computes unit price × quantity.
- Empty cart (spec 022): the page shows only "Your cart is empty" (Title M, `subtitle`) and the link "Browse coffees" to `/` (Button G style in `purple-dark`, underlined), at the left of the content; the design has no frame for it, so the look is proposed.
- Corners: buttons, counter, location pill, and payment options 6 px; inputs 4 px; tags 100 px; round icons 1000 px; coffee card 6/36/6/36 px (top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left); order summary card 6/44/6/44 px; form cards 6 px; success order card 6/36/6/36 px with a 1 px gradient border from `#DBAC2C` to `#8047F8`.
- No shadows. The theme removes every shadow utility (`shadow-*`, `drop-shadow-*`, `text-shadow-*`), so a shadow cannot be added by accident. The hero has soft, blurred yellow and purple shapes in the background.
- The tokens are in `apps/app/src/styles/theme.css` and are listed with their Tailwind names in [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md). Measures: page margin 160 px (`px-page`), content width 1120 px (`max-w-content`), navbar height 104 px (`h-navbar`). Borders are 1 px. Text sizes are in `rem` (px / 16), so the browser font-size setting is respected.
- Tablet and mobile layouts are not in the Figma; spec 027 derives them from the desktop design and keeps its colors, type styles, radii, and components. The owner approved the rules below on 2026-11-08.
  - **Breakpoints** (Tailwind's own): mobile below 640 px (`sm`), tablet from 640 px to 1023 px, desktop from 1024 px (`lg`). The desktop layout at 1440 px is unchanged.
  - **Page margin** (`px-page`, one variable set in `base.css`): 16 px on mobile, 32 px on tablet, and on desktop `clamp(32px, (100vw - 1140px) / 2, 160px)`. The content is at most 1120 px wide and centered, so the margin only has to leave that room: at 1440 px the content starts at x=160 as designed. Between 1024 px and about 1180 px the content is narrower than 1120 px and the desktop layouts share it (see below).
  - **Navbar:** one row at every width; the logo and the cart do not shrink, and the location pill text is cut with an ellipsis instead of wrapping.
  - **Hero:** below 1024 px the intro is above the cup image, both centered (the text too); the title is 40 px on tablet and 32 px on mobile (48 px on desktop); the benefits are two columns on tablet and one on mobile; the image scales down with its proportions. The vertical padding shrinks (40, 64, then 92 px).
  - **Coffee list:** the grid is as many 256 px columns, 32 px apart, as fit, centered: four in 1120 px, three between 1024 px and about 1180 px, two on a tablet, one on a phone. Below 1024 px the chips go under the title and wrap onto more rows; the space under the list shrinks (64 px).
  - **Checkout:** below 1024 px one column in the order address card, payment card, summary (the summary card fills the width). From 1024 px two columns share the width as 640 to 448 (`minmax(0, 640fr) minmax(0, 448fr)`, exactly 640 and 448 px at 1120 px of content). The address rows use fractions equal to the design's pixels (200, 348, 276, and 60, the State column at least 60 px) and shrink with the card; below 640 px every field has its own row and the three payment options stack (each still 51 px high). Cards and the summary have 24 px of padding on mobile, 40 px from 640 px. A summary row lets the counter and Remove wrap under the name when the card is narrow.
  - **Empty cart and not found:** the links are at least 24 px high (they were 22 px), at every width.
  - **Confirmation:** below 1024 px the card is above the illustration, the card fills the width up to 526 px, and the illustration scales down (at most 492 px, centered); from 1024 px they share the row as designed. The padding is 24 px on mobile.
  - **Targets:** every button, link, and input is at least 24×24 px at every width (WCAG 2.2, 2.5.8); a hidden radio counts through its label (51 px high).
  - Nothing is hidden on a small screen, and there is no mobile menu, bottom bar, or cart drawer.

## Accessibility

All color pairings MUST meet WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI controls). Check the design tokens that carry text on tinted backgrounds, such as `#C47F17` on `#F1E9C9`, before using them for small text. The palette of the design has pairs below these ratios (eight, listed with their ratios in [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md)); the owner decided to keep them unchanged. `e2e/accessibility.spec.ts` names the six text pairs and fails on any other low-contrast pair, so a new pair must meet the ratios.
